**This project is for education and research only.** It studies how well a statistical model can estimate cricket match outcomes. It is not a betting system, a tipping service, or financial advice.

## Betting and the law

- **Betting may be illegal where you live.**
  - In India, betting and gambling on sport are prohibited in most states under state gambling laws.
  - The Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025 bans online real-money games and their promotion.
  - Prediction markets such as Polymarket and Kalshi are restricted or unavailable in many countries, including India.
- **Nothing here encourages or facilitates betting.** No part of this repository recommends, promotes or links to any betting platform. Market prices were used only as a public benchmark for measuring the model's accuracy.
- **It is your responsibility** to know and follow the laws of your jurisdiction.

## No financial advice

- **The rupee figures are hypothetical.** They come from a simulation that assumes idealised conditions:
  - payouts at fair odds with no bookmaker margin;
  - no fees or taxes;
  - no limits;
  - no delay in placing bets.
- **Real-world results would be worse.** The simulation found no reliable way to make money.
- **Past performance does not predict future results.**
- **The model is wrong often,** including at high confidence.
